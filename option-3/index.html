FAQ

Good to know

Are the clothes made in South Africa?

Yes. Nixz supports local industry and all her manufacturing is done under one umbrella. She strives for consistency in her fit and quality, keeping it all proudly South African.

Why can’t I order online?

Nixz strives to build a personal relationship with every person that buys clothes from her. By building a relationship, Nixz is able to learn about the style of clothing her clients like, allowing her to design clothes that best fit her clients. You can place an order through our live chat or by phone, and we’ll look after you personally.

Do you have large stock of each item?

Stock is limited. If an item of clothing is in demand it can be re-stocked.

How many days do I have to pay and collect a product on hold?

Products that are put on hold and not paid for must be collected within 3 – 4 working days.

How long do I have to return an item?

Items can be returned within 14 days from the date of purchase. Please see our Return Policy for the full conditions.
