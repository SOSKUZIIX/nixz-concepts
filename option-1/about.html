Nicola Jaffar · Owner

Nixz, in her own style.

Nixz is a Durban-based entrepreneur, born in the 70s. She believes in free-spirited fashion and lives her life — and runs her business — with that philosophy in mind.

A passion for people.

Nixz believes that every woman needs to be authentic, natural and work on her self-esteem. Women create their own style as they mature and as their personalities develop. It is here that Nixz believes the confidence of women can build their future.

“If you look good, you feel good,” Nixz says.

She includes an essence of passion in her decision-making and sticks to her gut feeling in making it all come together. Quality, knowledge and well-being are always the subjects chatted about in her boutique.

All new samples are fit-tested by Nixz herself and approved. She loves sharing tips and referring her clients to respective health practitioners — her interest in and love for others knows no bounds.

Nixz is the brand and she lives it passionately. One shopping rendezvous with her and you’ll never forget her authenticity.

“A woman needs to be feminine at all times and a high self-esteem is always on trend.”

Where it all started

  1. 01

    The flea markets

    Nicola Jaffar started out in the flea markets, taking a chance with two thousand rand.

  2. 02

    A store of her own

    After eight years of dedication and a vision of owning her own store, she opened her first shop in La Lucia Mall.

  3. 03

    Image coaching

    Her passion for dressing women grew into one-on-one image coaching — make-up, colour coding and dressing your shape.

  4. 04

    Growing confidence

    Her loyal clients join her vision to grow another woman’s confidence by donating their good second-hand Nixz clothes.

Service that goes further

Nixz has a service level like no other — built on relationships, with many long-term clients as a result.

  • Hems adjusted at no charge

    Nixz adjusts hems for her clients at no charge.

  • Downsizing

    Nixz alters clothes for clients who have lost weight, in an effort to promote their self-esteem.

  • Fabric sourcing

    Special request? She sources fabrics to suit.

  • A full make-over

    She is also an image consultant — so when you buy a new outfit you can have an entire make-over.

  • Loyalty & coaching

    A discount loyalty card is available, as well as complimentary one-on-one image coaching for existing clients.

  • Referrals & personal shopping

    Nixz is a problem solver: she always has a referral on hand, or does personal shopping for a client.

Behind the scenes

Made locally, under one roof.

Behind the scenes Nixz is a family-run business. She is ably assisted by her husband, Shoban, kept busy by her three children and loved unconditionally by Paisley, the cat, whom she calls her muse.

Shoban and her small team of local machinists at her sewing room have been keeping the wheels turning for over 27 years. Everything is locally made under one roof using local talent, and her head machinist — her “go-to girl” — has been there since the beginning.

Nixz supports local industry and strives for consistency in her fit and quality, keeping it all proudly South African. Her vision is to stock other boutiques with her brand and pass on her knowledge.

Giving back

When you give with a warm heart, you open the doors.

Behind the scenes, Nixz dresses women who are struggling financially in good quality, second-hand clothing donated by volunteers clearing out their closets. It began one day when she was passing on her own seconds to a good woman.

Her loyal clients join in by donating their good second-hand Nixz clothes — in the form of a workshop for the ladies, not a knock-and-drop. It is part of well-being and nurturing as women to give.

Natural fibres

Drape, shape, never conform.

Nixz chooses to style with natural fibres that breathe, and her signature look is earthy. It’s about being grounded, never losing yourself, and giving yourself the chance to start over again and declutter at any point in time. Linens and cottons weave themselves into this belief.

The look and feel of the range is to drape, shape and never conform — allowing your attitude to flow in the elegance of daily wear.

“Natural fibres are anti-ageing… so wear your crease in your clothes and not on your face.”
