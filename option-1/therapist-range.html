Therapist Range

We offer a range of natural clothing to the therapist profession — calm, earthy pieces for a renewed sense of well-being at work.

Prices differ according to the size of the order and fabric choice. Contact Nicola Jaffar (Nixz), who will coordinate a range for your business requirements.

Enquire for your business See the range & prices

Why natural fibres

  • 01

    Made for movement

    Items are carefully chosen to suit a clean, simple and holistic outlook — made for the ease of working with clients and patients.

  • 02

    Natural fibres that breathe

    Natural fibres are healthier to work in: they absorb moisture and allow body heat to escape, unlike feeling trapped in synthetics.

  • 03

    Care advice included

    We’ll show you how to look after your natural fibres — they have an excellent lifespan.

  • 04

    From one therapist to a whole team

    Offered to any spa or therapy business, with a staff complement of as little as one.
