Where it all started

A boutique built on confidence.

Nixz is a Durban-based entrepreneur who believes in free-spirited fashion — and runs her business with that philosophy in mind.

She believes every woman needs to be authentic, natural and work on her self-esteem. Women create their own style as they mature and as their personalities develop — and it’s here that Nixz believes the confidence of women can build their future.

“If you look good, you feel good.”

Quality, knowledge and well-being are always the subjects chatted about in her boutique.
