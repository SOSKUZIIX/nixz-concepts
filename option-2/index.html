Where it all started

A boutique built on confidence

Nixz is a Durban-based entrepreneur who believes in free-spirited fashion, and runs her business with that philosophy in mind. She believes every woman needs to be authentic, natural and work on her self-esteem.

Women create their own style as they mature and as their personalities develop. It’s here that Nixz believes the confidence of women can build their future. “If you look good, you feel good.”

Service that goes further

Nixz adjusts hems at no charge, does downsizing for clients who have lost weight, sources fabrics for special requests and is an image consultant too. A discount loyalty card is available, as well as complimentary one-on-one image coaching for existing clients.

Made locally, under one roof

Behind the scenes Nixz is a family-run business. She is assisted by her husband, Shoban, and a small team of local machinists who have kept the wheels turning for over 27 years. Everything is made locally under one roof, keeping it all proudly South African.

Giving back

Nixz dresses women who are struggling financially in good quality, second-hand clothing donated by volunteers. Her loyal clients join in by donating their good second-hand Nixz clothes, as part of a workshop for the ladies.

Natural fibres, made locally

Nixz chooses to style with natural fibres that breathe, and her signature look is earthy: grounded, and never losing yourself. Linens and cottons that drape, shape and never conform, made under one roof by a small, loyal team and proudly South African.

The Therapist Range

Made for movement, made for care

Natural clothing for the therapist profession: clean, simple and holistic, with the ease of working with clients in mind. Natural fibres absorb moisture and allow body heat to escape, unlike feeling trapped in synthetics.

We offer advice on looking after your natural fibres, and the service is open to any spa or therapy business, from a team of one upwards. Prices differ according to the size of the order and fabric choice.
